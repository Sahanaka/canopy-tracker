# 0011 — Proposed composite windows and first Landsat year

- **Status:** proposed — awaiting owner acceptance
- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Task:** M1.1

## Context

M1.2 needs composite windows for Sentinel-2 and M1.5 needs the first year the Landsat record supports annual
composites. CLAUDE.md says both come from the availability audit, not from assumptions. The audit (ADR 0010; AOI of
ADR 0009; run 2026-11-02) gives, per window and year, the share of AOI pixels with zero clear observations, the
median per-pixel count of clear observations and the number of acquisitions. This ADR presents those tables, states the
rule used to read them as an explicit assumption, and proposes windows and a year. Nothing here is accepted until the
owner says so, and M1.1 stays unticked until then.

## Decision (proposed)

### Assumptions (the rule)

- **A1 Candidate windows** are the four Met Department seasons from CLAUDE.md: FIM Mar–Apr, SWM May–Sep, SIM Oct–Nov, NEM
  Dec–Feb (December counts toward the following year). No other splits were tested.
- **A2 Minimum for a window** is one clear observation for the median AOI pixel in each complete season-year. One
  observation is the logical minimum for a median composite; it is not a tuned value. No percentage cut-off on the
  zero-observation share is applied: those shares are reported so the owner can judge them.
- **A3 Evaluation years** for Sentinel-2 are 2016–2025 plus 2026 where the season is complete, because the first S2
  acquisition with data over the AOI is 2016-01-14 (2015 has none, see ADR 0010). SIM 2026 is partial (the run date is
  2026-11-02) and is excluded.
- **A4 First Landsat year** is the first calendar year Y such that every year from Y to the last complete year (2025)
  has at least one clear observation for the median pixel.
- **A5** The clear definition is `clear_base` for Sentinel-2 (SCL 4, 5, 6) and `clear` for Landsat (QA_PIXEL bits 0, 1, 3,
  4, 5, plus cirrus for L8/9). Choosing the SCL keep-set is M1.2's decision (ADR 0010); `clear_keep27` is shown below to
  bound its effect.

### Proposal 1 — Sentinel-2 windows for M1.2

Use all four Met Department seasons as windows. Under A2 every window passes in every evaluated season-year: the minimum
of the yearly median counts is 1 (FIM, NEM), 3 (SWM) and 2 (SIM). Ranked by the data (no cut-off), SWM is the most
reliable (median count 3–15, at most 0.23 % of pixels without a clear observation in any year), then NEM and FIM
(medians 1–9; FIM has the larger tail, up to 19.8 % in 2017), and SIM is the thinnest (median 2–4, up to 7.8 % of pixels
without a clear observation in a complete year). The thin season-years are FIM 2016 (median 1, 5.9 % zero), FIM 2017
(median 1, 19.8 %), FIM 2022 (4.4 %), SIM 2020 (6.1 %) and SIM 2023 (7.8 %). Whether a median of 1–2 observations is robust
enough, and whether to drop 2016–2017 (the single-satellite years: 18 and 26 valid acquisitions), is left to the owner; M1.2
should carry the per-pixel observation count with each composite so that low-count pixels are flagged and not silently
filled.

### Proposal 2 — first Landsat year for M1.5

**1999** under A4: 1998 has no acquisition at all, and every year from 1999 to 2025 has a median of at least 1 clear
observation per pixel. Two cautions go with it, and the owner may prefer **2000** (the first year with more than one
acquisition, 6 of them, and with 100 % of pixels observed):

- 1999 rests on a single Landsat 7 scene (98.88 % of pixels have one clear observation).
- From 2000 on every year has at least 99.87 % of pixels with a clear observation (worst: 2008 at 99.88 %, 2020 at 99.95 %, 2002 at 99.99 %).
- Before 1999 the record is discontinuous (Landsat 5 only): 1988 13 scenes, 1989 1, 1990 none, 1991 4, 1992 6, 1993 4, 1994–1997
  12–20, 1998 none; 1989, 1990 and 1998 have no usable year and 1991 and 1993 have 86.3 % and 81.6 % of pixels observed. So 1988–1997
  cannot form a gap-free annual series. Nothing is in the PC archive for this path/row before 1988-01-14.
- Sensor changes inside the proposed series: Landsat 5 and 7 both contribute in 2001 and 2003–2011, Landsat 7 alone 2012 (16 scenes, SLC-off),
  Landsat 7 and 8 in 2013, Landsat 8 alone 2014–2020, Landsat 9 joins in 2021. The harmonisation ADR is part of M1.5.

The Landsat seasonal picture is much thinner than Sentinel-2's: SIM (Oct–Nov) has a median of 0–3 observations and has
zero-observation shares above 50 % in several years, so seasonal Landsat composites for SIM would not support a median.
Annual composites (the M1.5 plan) are supported; a seasonal Landsat composite is not proposed.

## Consequences

- If accepted, M1.2 builds four composites per year from 2016 (SIM 2026 once complete), and M1.5 starts annual Landsat composites at
  1999 (or 2000).
- Rejecting any assumption (for example requiring a higher minimum count) changes the proposal; the per-pixel count rasters
  (`outputs/M1.1/clear_counts_*`) and the notebook let the tables be recomputed without re-reading any scene.
- Cloud-clear status here is whatever SCL and QA_PIXEL call clear. Neither is validated against ground truth, so
  composites may still contain thin cloud or shadow.
- Terrain shading is not modelled here; M1.3 supplies the per-scene shadow flag.
